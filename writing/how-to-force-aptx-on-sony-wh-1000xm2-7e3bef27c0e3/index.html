How to force aptX on Sony WH-1000XM2

It’s been quite some time since my last post, but this issue has been annoying and persistent enough to warrant a post on its own.

JJ Shaw · May 5, 2019 · 6.2K views

It’s been quite some time since my last post, but this issue has been annoying and persistent enough to warrant a post on its own.

If you own a pair of Sony WH-1000XM2 or WH-1000XM3, you might have inadvertently been listening to a degraded codec when you connect to a Mac.

My XM2 has been consistently connecting via the worse SBC codec instead of the higher definition aptX codec. To check what Bluetooth connection your pair of headphones have been using, Option + Click the Bluetooth connection icon on your Mac’s menu bar.

1. Open Terminal.app

2. Enter the following, followed by your Mac login password.

sudo defaults write bluetoothaudiod "Enable AptX codec" -bool true

3. Reconnect your Bluetooth headphones.
